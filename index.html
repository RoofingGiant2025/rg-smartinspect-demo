    About this SmartInspect demo

    Does this inspect or measure a real building?

    No. Both properties, their findings, quantities, and reference identifiers are fictional examples. This page cannot inspect a roof or produce a real roof measurement.

    Can I get a real estimate here?

    No. Example quantities are unpriced placeholders. A real quote requires an authorized inspection, verified measurements, materials, and the actual Roofing Giant sales process.

    What happens to information I enter?

    There is no account, contact form, or data submission. Checkpoint searches happen only in your current browser tab. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection logs under its own policies.

    Are these manufacturer installation instructions?

    No. The public demo intentionally withholds construction assemblies. Real installation details need current manufacturer literature and independent professional review.
